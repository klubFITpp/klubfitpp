Another FIT LAN Party is on its way! Mark November 4th in your calendar. 🎮

Enjoy an evening full of fun – play games with your friends and take part in GeoGuessr, TrackMania, and Beat Saber tournaments. Of course, there will be prizes for the winners!

Besides the tournaments, you can try VR and a racing wheel with pedals, join our Civilization 6 game or our Minecraft server. If you just want to relax, there'll also be board games, food, and drinks waiting for you. 🍺

We can't wait to see you there!
